# The Hurt Locker 1.1.0

- **Optional NPC recharge cards.** Enable **Include NPC recharge cards** in The Hurt Locker’s module settings to see the item name, whether it recharged, and the roll total in one compact row. The setting is off by default and applies per client. It picks up manual recharge rolls and automatic NPC recharge rolls posted to chat by dnd5e. Recharge and damage cards share the **Keep** limit.
- **More compact damage cards.** A small timestamp in the upper right replaces the sender and whisper header and the large HP status heading. Tighter padding gives the expanded target rows more room. The rows retain their damage and HP values and Midi-QOL’s apply, undo, and multiplier controls.

Use Foundry VTT **13.351 or later in generation 13**, dnd5e **5.2.5 or later**, and Midi-QOL **13.0.72 or later**.
