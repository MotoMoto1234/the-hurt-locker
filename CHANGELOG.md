# Changelog

## 1.0 - First public release (1.0.0)

- GM-only damage-card window with chat toggle and rebindable Alt+H shortcut.
- Configurable 3-10 card limit, saved position and size, and open-state restoration.
- Bounded incremental rendering, grouped updates, and safe asynchronous close/update handling.
- Native Midi-QOL apply, undo, and multiplier controls.
- Stay at the top on arrivals and updates when already there, without forcing readers back from older cards.
- Compact 24-pixel sender avatars and normal title-bar minimization/restoration.
- Dark steel and brass framing without custom animation or blur.

Includes all fixes from local development builds 1.0.1-1.0.3. Public versioning starts at 1.0.0.
